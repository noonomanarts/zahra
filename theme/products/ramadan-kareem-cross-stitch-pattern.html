Description

A cross stitch pattern of Ramadan Kareem – Digital PDF counted cross stich pattern download

This counted cross stitch chart by Zahra Allawati is ready to download now using Adobe reader, print it out at home or view on your device and start stitching today.

You will download 2 PDF files, each with different color pallet.

Chart Details:

  • Design size: 7.1×7.1 inch or 18×18 cm
  • Stitches: 100 × 100 (cross stitch & back stitch)
  • Fabric: 14 count aida

PDF File Includes:

  • Cover page with image of complete design
  • Solid block chart
  • Blocks and symbols chart
  • DMC thread and color list

You can stick to the color chart available, or create your own color chart.

If you have any questions about the pattern, please feel free to contact us, we’re happy to help.

Please do not sell, claim this pattern as your own, or use my photos.

Thank you & enjoy stitching.
